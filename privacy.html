Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Flatmate helps people who share a house or flat organise chores, money, plans and notices. This policy explains what we keep, who can see it and how to remove it. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.

What we keep

Your account: your email address (or phone number), your name, an optional profile picture, the colour you pick, your status and note, and any social usernames you add.

Your house: everything you and your housemates add to it, such as chores, expenses, bills, receipts, shopping, plans, photos, memories, notices, polls, reactions, suggestions, guests, house info, house rules and move-in checks.

Bank details, only if you add them: account name, sort code, account number and Monzo.me username, so housemates can pay you back.

Messages you send to the Flatmate team from Contact & suggestions, and any screenshots you attach to a bug report, with the app version and phone type.

Notification settings and reminder times. Some settings, such as your theme and bottom row, are only saved on your phone.

Who can see it

Things you add to a house are only visible to the people in that house. Bank details are only visible to your current housemates. Nobody outside your house can see your house's data.

Messages and screenshots sent to the Flatmate team are only seen by the team.

We use these services to run Flatmate: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in Ireland in the EU) and Google Gmail (to send sign-in code emails). They only process data to provide those services.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, show your house the things you share, work out who owes who, send reminders and notifications you've turned on, and fix problems you report. The legal basis is providing the service you've asked for (and, for bank details and photos, your choice to add them).

How long we keep it

Deleted chores, money items, notices and plans stay in Recently deleted for 7 days so they can be restored, then they're removed for good. Quick posts disappear after a few hours.

Everything else is kept while your account exists, or until the house is deleted.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Danger zone. This removes your sign-in, your details, your profile picture, your bank details, your move-in photos, your reactions and votes, and screenshots you've sent us.

So that your housemates' balances still add up, past expenses and payments you were part of stay, shown as "Former flatmate". If you were the last person in your house, the whole house is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how it's used. Email us and we'll reply within a month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Flatmate is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date below and mention it in the app.
