# Flatmate

**Chores, money, plans and notices for your shared house.**

Flatmate is a phone app for iPhone and Android that keeps a shared house or flat running smoothly, so nobody has to nag.

- **Chores that take turns**: weekly chores move on to the next person by themselves, and you can ask to swap.
- **Money without the awkwardness**: split costs equally, by amounts or by %, add receipts, and see who owes who in the fewest payments.
- **Plans and memories**: suggest a dinner or night out, everyone answers Going, Maybe or Can't, and photos end up in Memories.
- **Notices & suggestions**: notices with photos, polls, dates and emoji reactions, plus a suggestions box everyone can upvote.
- **Everything else**: a shared shopping list, house calendar, bin days, Wi-Fi and contacts, house rules, guests and move-in checks.

Only people in your house can see what you share. No ads, no tracking.
